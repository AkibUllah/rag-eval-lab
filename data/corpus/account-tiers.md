# Account tiers and balance caps

Shapla Pay offers two personal tiers. Your tier decides how much money you can keep in the wallet and which services you can use.

## Regular account

Every account starts as Regular after eKYC approval. A Regular account can hold a maximum balance of BDT 300,000. All everyday services are available: cash in, cash out, send money, payments, bill pay and mobile recharge.

## Premium account

Premium raises the maximum wallet balance to BDT 500,000 and unlocks Shapla Credit micro-loans and the savings scheme with higher monthly deposits. To upgrade, submit a utility bill or rental agreement as proof of address and declare your source of income in the app under Profile > Upgrade. Upgrades are reviewed within 3 working days.

## Business accounts

Shops and service providers should open a merchant account instead of using a personal wallet for business. Merchant accounts have separate limits that depend on the trade licence category.
