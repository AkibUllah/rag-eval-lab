# Cash out charges

Cash out means converting wallet balance into physical cash. The charge depends on where you cash out.

## Charge by channel

- At an agent, using the app: 1.39%, which is BDT 13.90 per BDT 1,000.
- At an agent, using the USSD menu (dial *268#): 1.49%, which is BDT 14.90 per BDT 1,000.
- At a partner bank ATM: 1.5%, with a minimum charge of BDT 10.

The charge is deducted from your wallet in addition to the cash out amount, so keep enough balance to cover it.

## Remittance cash out

Money received as an inward remittance from abroad can be cashed out at a reduced charge of 0.99% within 30 days of receipt.

## Agent overcharging

Agents must not ask for any extra money on top of the charge shown in the app. If an agent demands more, report it to customer support with the agent number from your SMS.
