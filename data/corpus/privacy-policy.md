# Privacy and data protection

## What we collect

We collect your NID details, selfie, mobile number, device information and transaction history to provide the service and to meet anti-money-laundering rules.

## How long we keep it

Transaction records and KYC documents are kept for 5 years after your account is closed, as required by regulation. After that they are deleted or anonymised.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with partner banks needed to complete a transaction, with regulators, and with law enforcement agencies when there is a lawful request.

## Marketing messages

You can stop promotional SMS and app notifications in Settings > Privacy > Marketing. Transaction and security messages cannot be switched off.

## Your rights

You can download a statement of your data and transactions from the app. To correct your name or date of birth, the change must first be made on your NID.
