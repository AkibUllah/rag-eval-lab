# Receiving remittance from abroad

Family members working abroad can send money directly to your Shapla Pay wallet through our partner exchange houses and money transfer apps in the Middle East, Malaysia, Singapore, the UK and the USA.

## Government incentive

Inward remittance through legal channels earns a 2.5% government cash incentive. The incentive is credited to your wallet automatically together with the remittance; you do not need to apply.

## Limits

A single remittance credit can be up to BDT 500,000. Remittance credits do not count against your cash in limit.

## Fees

Receiving remittance is free on the Shapla Pay side. The sender's exchange house may charge its own fee. Cashing out remittance money is charged at 0.99% for 30 days after receipt.
