# Contacting customer support

## Channels

- In-app chat under Help, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Hotline 09610-200300, from 8 AM to 10 PM every day, including public holidays.
- Email support@shaplapay.example for statements and written complaints.
- Service points in all 64 districts, open Saturday to Thursday, 10 AM to 6 PM.

## Response times

Chat messages are answered within 5 minutes on average. Emails are answered within 1 working day.

## Languages

Support is available in Bangla and English.

## Before you contact us

Keep your TrxID ready if the question is about a transaction. Support agents will verify your identity with your date of birth and recent transactions, never with your PIN.
