# Shapla Credit micro-loans

Shapla Credit gives small, short-term loans directly in the wallet, offered with our partner bank.

## Who is eligible

You need a Premium account that has been active for at least 6 months with regular transactions. Your loan limit is calculated from your transaction history and shown in the app under Loans.

## Amount and tenure

Loans range from BDT 500 to BDT 20,000. Choose a tenure of 30, 60 or 90 days.

## Cost

The service charge is a flat 1% for every 30 days of tenure, shown in full before you accept. There is no processing fee.

## Repayment

On the due date the amount is deducted from your wallet automatically. You can also repay early without penalty. A late payment fee of 0.1% per day is added after the due date, capped at 5% of the loan amount. Late repayment lowers your future loan limit.
