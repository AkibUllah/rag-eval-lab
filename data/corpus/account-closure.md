# Closing your account

You can close your Shapla Pay account at any time.

## How to close

Request closure in the app under Profile > Close account, or visit a service point with your NID. We call you to confirm the request.

## Remaining balance

Before closure, move any remaining balance to a bank account or another Shapla Pay wallet, or cash it out. Cash out of the closing balance is free when it is done as part of a closure request.

## When closure is not possible

An account cannot be closed while it has an outstanding Shapla Credit loan, an active savings scheme or an open dispute.

## Processing time

Closure requests are completed within 7 working days. After closure, the mobile number can be used to open a new account after 30 days.
