# Paying utility bills

Pay electricity, gas, water and internet bills from the Pay Bill menu.

## Supported billers

Electricity: DESCO, DPDC and the rural electrification prepaid meters. Gas: Titas Gas. Water: Dhaka WASA and Chattogram WASA. Internet: BTCL and several broadband providers listed in the app.

## Charges

Electricity bill payments are free. Gas and water bills have a 0.5% charge, capped at BDT 20 per bill. The charge is shown before you confirm.

## Receipts

A receipt is sent by SMS and saved in the app under Transactions. Billers usually update your account within 2 hours. Keep the receipt until the next bill shows the payment.

## Late payment

Paying after the due date is allowed, but the biller may add its own late fee. Shapla Pay cannot waive biller late fees.

## Failed bill payments

If money was deducted but the biller did not receive it, the amount is reversed automatically within 72 hours.
