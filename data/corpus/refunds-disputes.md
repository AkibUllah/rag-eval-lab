# Refunds and disputes

## Merchant refunds

Refunds for purchases are issued by the merchant. When a merchant refunds a payment, the money appears in your wallet instantly and you receive an SMS. Merchants can refund the full amount or part of it.

## Raising a dispute

If a transaction looks wrong, for example a payment you did not make or a merchant who did not deliver, raise a dispute in the app under Help > Report a transaction. Disputes must be raised within 30 days of the transaction date.

## What happens next

You receive an acknowledgement with a ticket number within 24 hours. We investigate with the merchant or receiving party and aim to resolve disputes within 7 working days. Cases involving another bank or a card network can take up to 45 days. If the dispute is decided in your favour, the money is credited to your wallet.

## What we need from you

The transaction ID (TrxID), the date and amount, and a short description. Screenshots of chats with the merchant help.
