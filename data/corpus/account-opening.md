# Opening a Shapla Pay account

Anyone aged 18 or older with a valid Bangladeshi National ID (NID) card or smart card can open a personal Shapla Pay account from the app. You need a mobile number registered in your own name.

## Steps

1. Install the Shapla Pay app and enter your mobile number.
2. Confirm the one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS.
3. Photograph the front and back of your NID card.
4. Take a live selfie. The app asks you to blink and turn your head to confirm you are present.
5. Set a 5-digit PIN.

## Verification time

Electronic KYC (eKYC) checks your NID data and selfie against the national database. Most accounts are approved automatically within 10 minutes. If the photo does not match or the NID data cannot be read, the application goes to manual review, which takes up to 2 working days. You will get an SMS with the result either way.

## Who cannot open an account

Applicants under 18 cannot hold a personal account. Foreign nationals need a business or remittance-only account, which is opened at a service point with a passport and a valid visa. One NID can be linked to only one personal account.
