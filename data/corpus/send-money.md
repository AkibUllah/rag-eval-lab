# Send money

Send money transfers balance from your wallet to another Shapla Pay wallet instantly.

## Fees

Sending up to BDT 100 is always free. For larger amounts the fee is BDT 5 per transaction. App users get 5 free send money transactions every calendar month regardless of amount; the free transactions are applied automatically.

## Sent money to the wrong number

Transfers are completed instantly and cannot be cancelled by you. Call customer support within 24 hours with the transaction ID (TrxID). If the money is still in the receiver's wallet, Shapla Pay can place a temporary hold while we contact the receiver, but the money is only returned with the receiver's consent or by a court or police instruction.

## Saved contacts

Add frequent receivers to your Priyo list to send money faster. Up to 10 Priyo numbers can be saved.
