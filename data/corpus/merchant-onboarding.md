# Merchant account onboarding

Shops, restaurants, online sellers and service providers can accept payments with a Shapla Pay merchant account.

## Documents required

- A valid trade licence in the business name
- The owner's NID
- A bank account in the business or owner's name for settlement
- A TIN certificate for businesses with expected annual sales above BDT 5,000,000

Online sellers without a trade licence can register as micro merchants with an NID and a link to their Facebook page or website.

## Approval

Applications submitted through the merchant portal are reviewed within 3 working days. We may visit the shop to verify the address.

## Settlement

Merchant collections are settled to the linked bank account on the next working day (T+1). Merchants can also choose instant settlement to their Shapla Pay merchant wallet at no extra charge.
