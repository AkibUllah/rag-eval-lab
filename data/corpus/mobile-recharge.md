# Mobile recharge

Top up any prepaid or postpaid number on Grameenphone, Robi, Airtel, Banglalink and Teletalk.

## Amounts

The minimum recharge is BDT 20 and the maximum is BDT 1,000 per recharge. You can buy internet and minute bundles directly from the Recharge screen.

## Cashback

You get 2% cashback on your first recharge of each day, up to BDT 10. Cashback is credited instantly.

## Recharged the wrong number

Recharges are sent to the operator immediately and cannot be reversed by Shapla Pay. Contact the mobile operator for help with a wrong recharge.

## Failed recharge

If the recharge failed but your balance was deducted, the money is returned automatically within 72 hours.
