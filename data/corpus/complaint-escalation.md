# Complaint escalation

If you are unhappy with how a problem was handled, you can escalate it in three levels.

## Level 1: Customer support

Raise the complaint through chat, the hotline or a service point. You get a ticket number. Most complaints are resolved within 7 working days.

## Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer

If the issue is not resolved, or you disagree with the outcome, email the Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance@shaplapay.example with your ticket number. The officer replies within 10 working days.

## Level 3: Regulator

If your complaint is still unresolved 30 days after you first raised it, you can take it to the central bank's customer protection desk. Include your ticket number and the officer's reply.
