# PIN and account security

Your 5-digit PIN authorises every transaction. Nobody from Shapla Pay will ever ask for it.

## Wrong PIN attempts

After 3 wrong PIN attempts, your account is locked for 24 hours. The lock lifts automatically, or you can unlock it sooner by resetting your PIN.

## Resetting a forgotten PIN

Tap Forgot PIN on the login screen. Enter the last 4 digits of your NID number and your date of birth, confirm the OTP sent to your registered number and take a selfie. The new PIN is active immediately. If the selfie check fails, visit a service point with your NID.

## Good practice

Change your PIN every 90 days, do not use your birth year or repeated digits such as 11111, and turn on fingerprint or face login in Settings > Security.

## Lost phone

If your phone is lost or stolen, call the hotline immediately to block the account. Your balance stays safe and is available again after you reactivate the account on a new device.
