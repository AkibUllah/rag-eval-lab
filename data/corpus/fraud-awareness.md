# Avoiding fraud and scams

Most fraud happens when customers are tricked into sharing their PIN or OTP.

## Common scams

- Callers pretending to be Shapla Pay staff who say your account will be closed unless you share an OTP.
- Messages claiming you won a prize and must pay a "processing fee" first.
- Someone who says they sent money to you by mistake and asks you to return it, when no money arrived. Always check your balance in the app, not the SMS they forward.

## Rules to remember

Shapla Pay staff never ask for your PIN or OTP. Official SMS come only from the sender name ShaplaPay. Never install screen-sharing apps at a caller's request.

## Reporting fraud

Call the hotline at 09610-200300 or use Help > Report fraud in the app. When you report fraud, we freeze the suspected receiving wallet within 15 minutes if the money has not been withdrawn. Also file a general diary (GD) at your local police station; we share transaction records with the police on request.
