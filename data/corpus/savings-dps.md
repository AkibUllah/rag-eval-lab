# Monthly savings scheme (DPS)

Shapla Savings is a deposit pension scheme (DPS) run with our partner bank. You save a fixed amount every month and receive the total with profit at maturity.

## Monthly deposit

Choose a monthly instalment between BDT 500 and BDT 10,000. Premium account holders can choose up to BDT 25,000. The instalment is deducted from your wallet on the same date every month.

## Tenure and profit

Available tenures are 1, 2, 3 and 5 years. The profit rate is 7% per year for 1 and 2 year schemes and 7.5% per year for 3 and 5 year schemes. Source tax on profit is deducted at maturity.

## Missed instalments

If your wallet does not have enough balance, the app retries for 3 days. Missing 3 consecutive instalments closes the scheme early.

## Early closure

You can close a scheme early after 6 months. You receive all deposits plus half of the profit earned so far. Closing before 6 months returns the deposits only.
