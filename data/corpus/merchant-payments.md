# Paying at shops with QR

You can pay at any shop that displays a Shapla Pay or Bangla QR code.

## How to pay

Open the app, tap Scan QR, scan the shop's code, enter the amount and confirm with your PIN. The shop gets an instant notification. You can also pay online merchants through the Shapla Pay checkout page.

## Fees for customers

Customers never pay a fee for merchant payments. If a shop asks you to pay extra for using Shapla Pay, please report it to customer support.

## Fees for merchants

Merchants pay a merchant discount rate (MDR) of 1.2% per payment. Micro merchants, meaning shops with monthly payment volume below BDT 100,000, pay a reduced MDR of 0.8%.

## Cashback offers

Cashback campaigns are announced in the app's Offers tab. Cashback is credited within 3 working days after the qualifying payment.
