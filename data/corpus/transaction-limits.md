# Transaction limits

Limits protect you and are set according to central bank guidelines for mobile financial services. They apply to both Regular and Premium accounts unless stated otherwise.

## Send money

You can send up to BDT 25,000 in a single transaction. The daily limit is BDT 50,000 across a maximum of 25 transactions, and the monthly limit is BDT 300,000.

## Cash out

You can cash out up to BDT 30,000 per day in at most 5 transactions. The monthly cash out limit is BDT 150,000.

## Cash in

Cash in is limited to BDT 30,000 per day in at most 5 transactions, and BDT 200,000 per month.

## Payments and bill pay

Merchant payments and bill payments have no separate daily count limit, but each payment is capped at BDT 100,000.

## When you hit a limit

Limits reset at midnight for daily limits and on the first day of the calendar month for monthly limits. Limits cannot be raised temporarily on request.
