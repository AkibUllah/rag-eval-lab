# Dormant accounts

An account becomes dormant when there has been no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months. Incoming money such as remittance or salary still arrives in a dormant account, but outgoing transactions are blocked.

## Reactivating

Open the app and follow the reactivation prompt: take a fresh selfie and confirm the OTP. Reactivation is usually instant. If the selfie check fails, visit a service point with your NID.

## Balance in dormant accounts

Your balance is kept safe and no dormancy fee is charged.
