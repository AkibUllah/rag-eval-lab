# Failed and pending transactions

Sometimes a transaction fails because of a network problem after your balance was deducted.

## Automatic reversal

Shapla Pay checks every incomplete transaction. If a transaction did not complete, the deducted amount is reversed to your wallet automatically within 72 hours, including any charge. You will get an SMS when the reversal is done.

## Pending status

A transaction shown as Pending is still being confirmed with the other party. Do not repeat the transaction while it is pending, or you may be charged twice.

## If the money is not returned

If the reversal has not arrived after 72 hours, raise a dispute from the transaction details screen with the TrxID. Failed transactions do not count against your daily limit once they are reversed.
