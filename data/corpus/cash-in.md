# Adding money (cash in)

There are three ways to add money to your Shapla Pay wallet.

## At an agent

Give cash to any Shapla Pay agent and they will transfer the amount to your wallet. Cash in at an agent is free. Always check that the confirmation SMS shows the correct amount before you leave the shop.

## From a bank account

Link your bank account in the app under Add Money > Bank. Transfers from partner banks are free and usually arrive within a few seconds; transfers from other banks through the national payment switch can take up to 30 minutes.

## From a debit or credit card

You can add money from a Visa or Mastercard card. Adding money from a card has a 1% fee, which is shown before you confirm. Card top-ups require 3-D Secure verification.

Daily and monthly cash in limits are listed on the transaction limits page.
