# Linking debit and credit cards

You can link Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards issued in Bangladesh to add money to your wallet.

## Adding a card

Go to Add Money > Card > Add new card and enter the card number, expiry date and CVV. Your bank sends a 3-D Secure OTP to confirm the card belongs to you. A small amount may be held and released to check the card.

## Limits

Up to 3 cards can be linked to one account. Cards in another person's name cannot be linked.

## Fees

Adding money from a card costs 1% of the amount. Removing a card is free and can be done at any time.

## Card security

Shapla Pay stores only a token from the card network, never the full card number or CVV.
